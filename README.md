# Connect & Create

Website Brief — Consulting & Business Matching Company

1. Objective

Build a modern, minimal, professional website for [COMPANY NAME], a consulting company operating across two main business areas:

Products — software products and digital platforms we have developed.

Services — an AI-powered business matching service connecting companies looking for solutions with qualified service providers.

The website must immediately communicate these two distinct areas and make it extremely easy for visitors to choose the relevant path.

All website copy, navigation, buttons, forms, and UI elements must be in English.

2. Design Direction

Create a website that feels:

Modern

Minimalist

Premium

Professional

Technology-driven

Clean and highly organized

Trustworthy

Easy to navigate

Use generous white space, strong typography, subtle animations, simple visual elements, and a restrained color palette.

Avoid visual clutter, excessive gradients, overly complicated illustrations, or a generic corporate-consulting appearance.

The website should feel like a modern technology and advisory company rather than a traditional consulting firm.

The design should be fully responsive and optimized for desktop and mobile.

3. Website Structure

Create three main pages:

Home

Products

Services

Include a persistent top navigation bar and a professional footer.

Header Navigation

Include:

Logo / Company Name

Home

Products

Services

Contact

The two primary navigation elements should be Products and Services.

4. Homepage

Hero Section

Create a concise hero section that explains that [COMPANY NAME] builds digital products and connects businesses with the right partners.

Suggested positioning:

Headline:
"Building products. Connecting businesses."

Subheadline:
"We develop digital products and help companies find the right service providers through intelligent, AI-powered matching."

Include two prominent CTA buttons:

Explore Our Products →
Links to /products

Discover Our Services →
Links to /services

The visual hierarchy should make these two business areas immediately understandable.

Two Core Areas

Below the hero, create two large visual sections or cards.

01 — Our Products

Explain that we design, build, and launch software products and digital platforms.

Short example copy:

"We build and launch digital products designed to solve real business problems. Explore the software platforms and projects we have developed."

CTA:

View Our Products →

02 — Our Services

Explain that we connect companies looking for specialized expertise with qualified service providers.

Short example copy:

"We help businesses find the right partners — from software providers and marketing agencies to legal advisors and M&A specialists — using AI-powered matching."

CTA:

Find the Right Partner →

5. Products Page

Create a clean portfolio-style page showcasing the different software products and digital projects developed by the company.

Page Header

Our Products

"Explore the digital products, platforms, and software projects we have built."

Product Layout

Create a vertically scrollable portfolio.

Each product should have its own section or card containing:

Product name

Logo or product image

Short description

Problem the product solves

Optional category/tag

Link to the live platform

CTA:

Visit Platform →

Use placeholder products initially so they can easily be replaced later.

Example structure:

[PRODUCT NAME]

[Short description explaining what the platform does, who it is designed for, and the problem it solves.]

Visit Platform → [URL]

Repeat this structure for multiple products.

Make the architecture modular so new products can easily be added in the future.

6. Services Page

This page should explain the company's AI-powered business matching service.

The company acts as an intermediary between:

Demand:
Companies looking for specialized services or solutions.

Supply:
Agencies, consultants, software providers, professional firms, and other service providers looking for qualified business opportunities.

The platform/company uses AI to identify potential matches between business requirements and qualified providers.

7. Services — Hero Section

Suggested supporting copy:

"We connect companies looking for specialized expertise with qualified service providers. Tell us what you need — or what you offer — and our AI-powered matching process will identify relevant opportunities."

Immediately present two distinct paths:

I'm Looking for a Service

For businesses searching for a provider.

CTA:

I'm a Service Provider

Add a simple visual three-step section.

13. UX Requirements

The website should make the company's two business areas understandable within a few seconds.

A first-time visitor should immediately understand:

A. "They build software products."

and

B. "They connect businesses with service providers."

The user should never have to search for the relevant action.

Keep the primary conversion paths visible throughout the website:

Business looking for a provider → Submit requirements

Service provider looking for clients → Join provider network

Use clear CTA buttons and avoid unnecessary navigation complexity.

14. Technical & Visual Requirements

Fully responsive design

Mobile-first optimization

Fast loading

SEO-friendly structure

Semantic HTML

Accessible navigation and buttons

Smooth but subtle animations

Modern typography

Generous spacing

Reusable product cards

Reusable service/provider components

Easy addition of future products

Easy replacement of form URLs

Strong CTA hierarchy

Minimal use of decorative elements

Professional micro-interactions and hover states

Do not overdesign the interface.

Prioritize clarity, credibility, simplicity, and conversion.

15. Core Brand Message

The entire website should communicate one simple idea:

We build technology and create valuable business connections.

Products demonstrate what we build.

Services use technology, expertise, and AI-powered matching to connect companies with the right partners.
